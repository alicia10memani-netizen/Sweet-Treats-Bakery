About Sweet Treats Bakery

Sweet Treats Bakery is a small bakery that provides freshly baked products for our cutomers.

We make cakes, cupcakes, cookies, muffins, pastries and other sweet treats. We also create custom cakes for birthdays, weddings, celebrations and other special occasions.

Our Goal

Our goal is to provide fresh and tasty baked products while giving our customers friendly and helpful service.
